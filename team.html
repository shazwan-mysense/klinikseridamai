(02) How we read your results

Three readings, one physician.

01

Screening

Dr Adeyemi reads every MRI, ultrasound and DEXA himself, usually within two days, and writes his findings in plain English.

02

Health screening

Dr Valkonen and Sam Okoro review your bloods, fitness and strength against your age, your history and your last visit.

03

The same doctors every visit

Your named physician reads everything, writes your three priorities on page two of the report and sits down with you for an hour.
